With the team of the study on automated lucid dreaming induction, I am also developing an overview of methodological best practices to conduct at-home sleep studies. This work is being developed into a first-author manuscript, aimed at helping other researchers implementing similar studies. It covers the practical aspects that are essential for successful data collection, i.e. recommendations for hardware and software; guidance on participant instruction and support; scheduling tools; advice regarding (remote) data quality monitoring and troubleshooting common issue. I also share this practical expertise through workshops, guest lectures and hands-on training of our lab rotation students (see [here](https://m-minnema.nl/events/)).
