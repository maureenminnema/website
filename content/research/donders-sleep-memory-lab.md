As a research assistant in the Sleep & Memory Lab of the Donders Institute for Brain, Cognition and
Behaviour, I support research into how sleep shapes memory and conscious experience, from the role
of sleep in dementia to lucid dreaming: dreams in which you know that you are dreaming.
