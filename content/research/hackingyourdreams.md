I co-authored an infographic published in the _Cognitive Science Student Journal_ in 2024 ([accessbile online](https://cogsci-journal.uni-osnabrueck.de/imagine-hacking-your-own-dreams/)), in which we explored the possibility of influencing dream content. The aim was to translate recent developments in sleep and dream research into an accessible and visually engaging format for a broader audience. This project is a good representation of my skills in visual communication and information design.
