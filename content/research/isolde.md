The ISOLDE project (see [website](https://dreslerlab.github.io/ISOLDE/) is an international research initiative coordinated by our lab at the Donders Institute in Nijmegen, aimed to collect data of experienced lucid dreamers from six continents, thus creating the largest EEG dataset on lucid dreaming so far. I contribute to the practical coordination of the study in the Netherlands, i.e. participant coordination and logistics; and quality control of the two-channel EEG recordings, as well as support the organization of data collection across the collaborating sleep laboratories internationally.
