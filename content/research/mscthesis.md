For my Master's thesis, I joined the lab of Prof. Donner at the University Medical Center Hamburg-Eppendorf where I in investigated the neural dynamics associated with episodic memory. Inspired by research demonstrating rapid replay during encoding and retrieval of episodic content, I explored whether associated high-frequency bursts could be identified in the MEG data of specific brain regions. To this aim, I learned to do pre-processing, source-localization, time-frequency analysis and cluster-based permutation testing with our pilot data.
