At the Donders Institute for Brain, Cognition and Behavior, I contribute to university teaching, including practical sessions on wearables for master's courses and workshops. I furthermore regularly represent our lab in interviews with secondary-school students working on their final-year research projects, and students of all levels, introducing them to sleep and (lucid) dreaming research). I have also taught seminars at the University of Osnabrück in 2023 (course: "The (cognitive) science of peace") and the University of Amsterdam in 2022 (course: "Cognition"), supporting group discussions, course organisation, and assessment. A selection of these activities is presented below. Beyond university teaching, I have around four years of tutoring experience across natural sciences and all secondary-school levels in the Netherlands. I have also led science and coding activities at summer camps for primary-school children and completed a youth leader qualification course.
