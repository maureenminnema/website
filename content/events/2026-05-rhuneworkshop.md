As part of the early-career consortium of the Germany-based Research Hub on Neuroethics (RHUNE), I was invited to speak at their first Future Workshop, organized in collaboration with NeurotechEU. The 2-day workshop was titeld: “Neuroethics in Transition: Challenges and Opportunities for Research, Policy, and Society.” I delivered a short presentation on the ethical questions I come across in my daily work in sleep research, especially regarding the use of wearable neurotechnologies.
