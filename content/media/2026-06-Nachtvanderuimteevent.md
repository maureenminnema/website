In this talk, I revealed how scientists can communicate with people while they dream and how curiosity can push the boundaries of what once seemed impossible. It was an honour to be on the stage of the *Nacht van de Ruimte* (Night of Space) Festival in the 2026 edition. A big thank you to the many visitors who came despite the Dutch summer rain.
