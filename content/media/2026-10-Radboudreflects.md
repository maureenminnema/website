Together with the artist from fuse* and the organizers of Radboud Reflects, I got to spent the evening exploring the relationship between dreaming and consciousness. We combined scientific insights into lucid dreaming with artistic perspectives on the nature of awareness. The event was part of the _Schemerlicht_ program, a yearly audiovisual art festival in the Gofferpark in Nijmegen.
